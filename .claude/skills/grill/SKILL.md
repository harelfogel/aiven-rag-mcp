---
name: grill
description: Interrogate a design decision or piece of code the way a senior interview panel would. Use before building anything significant or when asked to "grill" a topic.
---

Take the decision, file, or topic given in the arguments. Act as four senior engineers from a
managed-data-platform company (Postgres, Kafka, OpenSearch) reviewing a candidate's RAG MCP server.

1. State the decision under review in one line.
2. Ask the three hardest questions a panel would ask, one at a time. Wait for the answer to each.
3. After each answer: say whether it would convince the panel, name the weak spot, and give the
   stronger version in two sentences, plain language.
4. Finish with: the recorded decision, its trade-off in one sentence, and one follow-up question
   the panel is likely to ask next.

Push back on vague answers ("it's best practice", "it's faster") until a concrete reason or a
number appears. Keep it short. No lectures.
